# Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales data to identify key business trends, profitability patterns, product performance, and regional sales performance.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to help business stakeholders monitor KPIs and make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify high-performing product categories
- Analyze regional sales performance
- Identify top-performing products
- Understand monthly sales trends
- Analyze the impact of discounts on profitability
- Provide actionable business recommendations

## 📊 Dataset

The project uses the Superstore retail sales dataset containing sales, profit, customer, product, category, region, shipping, discount, and quantity information.

Dataset size:

- 9,994 records
- 21 original columns

Additional analytical columns were created during preprocessing.

## 📈 Key KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### Category Analysis
Analyzed sales and profit performance across different product categories.

### Regional Analysis
Compared sales and profitability across geographical regions.

### Product Analysis
Identified top-performing products based on sales.

### Time Analysis
Analyzed sales trends over time to identify changes in business performance.

### Discount Analysis
Evaluated the relationship between discount levels and profitability.

## 💡 Business Insights

- Identified major revenue-generating product categories.
- Compared profitability across product categories.
- Identified high-performing regions and products.
- Observed changes in sales performance over time.
- Analyzed the effect of discounts on profitability.

## 💼 Business Recommendations

- Focus inventory and marketing efforts on high-performing categories.
- Optimize discount strategies to protect profit margins.
- Review low-profit and loss-making products.
- Use seasonal sales patterns for inventory and marketing planning.
- Monitor regional performance to identify growth opportunities.
